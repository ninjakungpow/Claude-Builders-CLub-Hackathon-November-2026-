Researched 4 Nov 2026. Each fact is marked with how reliable it is:

- **[OFFICIAL]**: from Anthropic or the event's own page

## Theme and tracks [OFFICIAL]
Most events use the theme "Machines of Loving Grace", from Dario Amodei's essay about AI making life better.

1. Biology & Physical Health: make healthcare more accessible
2. Neuroscience & Mental Health: expand access to mental health support
3. Economic Empowerment & Education: remove barriers to learning and opportunity
4. Governance & Collaboration: help people take part in democracy and work together
5. Creative Flourishing: amplify creativity and help people find meaning

## Judging rubric [OFFICIAL – Cambridge, Imperial, ALU]
| Criterion | Points | Judges ask |
|---|---|---|
| Technical execution | 30 | Does it work? Is it well built? Is AI used well? |
| Impact potential | 25 | Is it a real problem? Who benefits? Could it scale? |
| Ethical alignment | 25 | Does it respect human dignity, address potential harms and expand access fairly? |
| Presentation | 20 | Can you clearly explain what you built and why it matters? |

Other events also judge on:
- **Use of Claude:** Claude must be core to the project, "not just a chatbot wrapper" (ALU, Cornell).
- **Creativity and novelty** (Cornell).
- **Ethics write-up:** a 1–2 page write-up on risks and limits (UC San Diego).

Typical submission: a 3–5 minute demo video, a public GitHub repo, a live URL and a README covering the problem, the solution, how Claude is used and the impact.

## Past winners
- **Cambridge, Mar 2026 [OFFICIAL]:**
  - Pathfinder: career paths from GCSEs to PhD

  - ReuniteUS: connects citizens and government

- **UT Austin [NEWS]:**
  - Chiwo: built in about an hour

- **Stanford [NEWS]:** Crucible, multi-agent AI that challenges your decisions

## Why they won [MY READ – judges didn't publish reasons]
1. A specific person with a specific problem
2. AI supports the person rather than replacing them
3. Civic and democracy themes win often
4. A working demo beats an ambitious one that's broken
5. More than a chatbot (multi-agent, real data)
6. Fun and warm ideas can win (Creative Flourishing)

## Anthropic ethos [OFFICIAL – anthropic.com/company]
- Mission: "ensure the world safely makes the transition through transformative AI"
- Act for the global good
- Hold light and shade (take both risks and benefits seriously)
- Be good to our users
- Ignite a race to the top on safety
- Do the simple thing that works
- Be helpful, honest, and harmless
- Put the mission first
